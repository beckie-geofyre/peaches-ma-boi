Connecting infrastructure, resources and opportunities

Submarine cable route engineering, desktop studies and permit feasibility for telecommunications and power interconnector projects, and connecting clients with the people their projects need.

Services

From desktop study to installation-ready route

Cable route desktop studies

Route options assessed against seabed geology, geohazards, environmental constraints, fishing and shipping activity, and third-party infrastructure, with a risk assessment for each route option.

Outputs DTS report, route options, risk assessment, hazard maps, landfall assessments

Route engineering

Route design and refinement for fibre-optic and power cables, using survey data and ArcGIS Pro and MAKAI Plan.

Outputs Route position lists (RPL), straight-line diagrams (SLD)

Permit feasibility studies

Permitting requirements researched jurisdiction by jurisdiction, with liaison with ministries, coastal authorities and maritime agencies.

Outputs Permit matrices, decision flow charts, PFS report

Book an appointment to view the interactive permit database

Site visits

Landing site and landfall visits to check beach conditions, access, existing infrastructure and local constraints on the ground.

Outputs Site visit reports, photo logs, landing site recommendations

GIS and spatial data

GIS project packages and spatial databases built and maintained over the project lifecycle.

Outputs ArcGIS Pro packages, charts, data QC

Resources

Connecting clients with the people their project needs, such as survey contractors, cable suppliers, permitting agents, local partners and specialist consultants.

Outputs Introductions, shortlists of contractors and partners
